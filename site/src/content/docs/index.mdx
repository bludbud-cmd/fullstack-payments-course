## How the course works

You learn in 10 parts and 37 modules, from how apps work to deploying your own. Every lesson explains one idea in plain language, shows it in working code, and ends with an exercise you do yourself. Every module ends with a mini-project and a short quiz.

The course is self-paced and free. All you need is a Mac and some patience.
