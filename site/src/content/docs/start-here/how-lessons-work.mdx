Every lesson follows the same shape, so you always know where you are.

1. **Goal.** One sentence about what you will be able to do after the lesson.
2. **Why it matters.** Where this shows up in a real app, usually in PayDeck.
3. **The idea.** A plain-language explanation, often with an everyday comparison.
4. **Worked example.** Real code, explained line by line. Type it yourself instead of copying it, because typing is how it sticks.
5. **Try it.** An exercise for you to do.
6. **Common mistakes.** The errors beginners usually hit, and what they mean.
7. **Recap.** A few points to come back to later.

## Exercises

Exercises look like the one below. Try the task on your own first. If you get stuck, open one hint at a time. Only open the solution once you have had a real go.

## Tracking your progress

At the end of each lesson, click **Mark this lesson as finished**. A check mark appears in the sidebar, and your [progress page](../../reference/your-progress/) lists everything you have finished.
