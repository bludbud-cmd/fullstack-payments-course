Every programmer gets stuck every day. Being stuck does not mean you are bad at this. It means you are doing it. What matters is having a routine for getting unstuck.

## The debugging checklist

<Steps>

1. **Read the error message, slowly.** Most errors say what went wrong and on which line. Look for a file name and a line number.

2. **Check the usual suspects.** A missing bracket or quote, a typo in a name, a capital letter where there should not be one, or a file that was not saved.

3. **Compare with the course code.** Every lesson links to the finished code in the course repo. Put yours side by side with it and look for differences.

4. **Undo until it works.** Go back to the last version that worked, then redo your changes one at a time.

5. **Search the exact error message.** Copy the main line of the error, without your own file names, and search for it.

6. **Take a break.** Seriously. A short walk solves a surprising number of bugs.

7. **Ask for help, well.** Say what you were trying to do, what you expected, what happened instead, the exact error message, and what you have already tried.

</Steps>
